# HR-Analytics-Employee-Attrition-Performance

This dataset is designed for Human Resources (HR) analytics and employee performance evaluation. It combines employee demographic information, job-related characteristics, performance review results, satisfaction levels, and educational backgrounds to help organizations analyze workforce performance, employee engagement, and retention.

Task to do :

- How employee satisfaction affects performance ratings.
- The relationship between training opportunities and job performance.
- Factors contributing to employee attrition.
- The impact of education, tenure, salary, and work-life balance on employee success.
- Differences in performance and satisfaction across departments and demographic groups.

## Data Structure
The Dataset consist of 5 tables consisting of employee, performance rating, education level, rating level, satisfaction level. The whole dataset have a 6899 total records.
